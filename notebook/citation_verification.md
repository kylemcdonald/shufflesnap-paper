# Citation verification notes

Verified 2026-09-23 for `paper/refs.bib` (53 entries). Bibliographic fields were checked against DOI-resolver metadata (doi.org content negotiation and the Crossref API, i.e. the publisher's own deposit), plus publisher and author pages, arXiv, JMLR, the NeurIPS proceedings, and official GitHub, PyPI and documentation pages. Fields that could not be confirmed were left out of the `.bib` file rather than guessed.

**Access limits during verification:** dblp (bot challenge), ACM DL (HTTP 403), IEEE Xplore (bot check), nowpublishers.com and SIAM epubs (403), and hal.science pages (bot challenge) could not be read directly. For those entries I used Crossref/doi.org metadata, the HAL API, author PDFs, and OpenAlex/Semantic Scholar abstracts. The only effect is that three ACM article numbers remain unverified (see "partial" rows).

## Findings relevant to point-to-grid layout

- **BSP-OT reports no experiments on uniform grid targets.** Its bijection benchmarks use 2D/3D point clouds (stippled images, disk to two disks, shapes), with ground truth from Bonneel et al.'s 2011 network simplex. It compares against an ε-auction solver (hera) only for persistence-diagram matching, where it reports a 52× speedup at under 1% error. Its stippling application places the target measure on pixel centers, but with non-uniform, brightness-based masses. Its color transfer permutes pixels in color space, not grid space. With k = 64 it reports typically under 1% relative error.
- **IsoMatch uses a dense Hungarian solve** (O(n³)) on unsquared Euclidean costs from Isomap coordinates to target slots, and names the minimum bijection as its scalability bottleneck.
- **LAS uses a dense, full-grid Jonker–Volgenant LAP** at every iteration, but between high-dimensional feature vectors and smoothed map vectors, not between 2D points and cells. FLAS drops the global LAP. The same paper states that "any linear assignment scheme like the Jonker-Volgenant Algorithm can be used to map the projected 2D positions to the best grid positions."
- **DGrid, SSM, RasterFairy and Gridfit use no LAP.** They rely on recursive partitioning or swapping heuristics. RasterFairy's current README (updated July 2026) claims that in benchmarks it "stays within a few percent of the exact solution on neighborhood preservation while running about 200× faster", and quotes "about 30 seconds and a lot of RAM for 4,096 points" for an exact solve.
- **SciPy vs. OR-Tools.** SciPy's `linear_sum_assignment` is Crouse's modified JV without initialization (C++ since 1.4.0). OR-Tools' assignment and min-cost-flow solvers are Goldberg-style cost-scaling push-relabel and take **integer** costs (`CostValue` is `int64_t`), so squared distances must be scaled and rounded.
- **Practitioner evidence for "t-SNE → grid via dense LAP":** the src-d/lapjv README, the 2017 source{d} blog post, and CloudToGrid (2016–2017).
